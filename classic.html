05Questions

Before you register

71 answers about BitFest and each contest. Can't find yours? Write to csefest@kuet.ac.bd.

  • University students from across Bangladesh, and school students in the ICT Olympiad. Some contests are individual (the two online AI competitions and the ICT Olympiad); teams run from two members (Three-Minute Thesis) to four (Soccer Bot and Capture the Flag). IUPC teams of three bring a coach, and esports entries go from solo FIFA and Clash Royale players to Valorant teams of five.

  • The onsite finals are on Friday 5 and Saturday 6 February 2027 on the KUET campus in Khulna, and CSE Night closes the festival on the evening of the 6th. Online rounds run before that, from 20 to 29 January.

  • Registration opens on 7 December 2026 and closes on 19 January 2027. Fees are per team or per person: BDT 350 to 500 per person for the individual contests, BDT 1,000 to 2,000 per team for most others and BDT 5,500 for IUPC. The AI Hackathon and Capture the Flag preliminaries are free to enter.

  • From 20 to 29 January, the AI Hackathon, Datathon and Capture the Flag run online preliminaries and the Tech Business Case Competition a written round. The top 40, 30, 40 and 10 teams come to KUET for the finals, announced on 31 January. AI-Driven Solutions for Bangladesh's Needs and Imagine the Future with AI are online only: entries are due on 25 January.

  • Yes, two. AI-Driven Solutions for Bangladesh's Needs (a poster) and Imagine the Future with AI (a written piece) are individual online competitions with entries due on 25 January 2027. Their winners are announced at the closing ceremony on 6 February.

  • The Department of Computer Science and Engineering at KUET. Each competition is run by a student club or society under faculty supervision: SGIPC runs IUPC; IEEE Computer Society AI-Driven Solutions and the ICT Olympiad; Bit2Byte the AI Hackathon; KMiNDS Three-Minute Thesis and the Datathon; HACK the Project Showcase, Line-Following Robot and Soccer Bot; KCSC Capture the Flag; and the BitFest team Imagine the Future with AI, the Tech Business Case Competition and Esports Gaming. Write to csefest@kuet.ac.bd.
