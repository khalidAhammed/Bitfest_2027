Find a real problem in Bangladesh and propose a practical AI solution to it. An individual online poster competition: posters are due on 25 January, and the winners are announced at the closing ceremony on 6 February.

01About the contest

Notice. Propose. Present.

Participants identify real-life problems, unmet needs or opportunities for improvement in Bangladesh and propose practical AI-driven solutions. Working prototypes are not required. It runs online as an individual poster presentation, organised by the IEEE Computer Society: posters are due by 25 January 2027, and the winners are announced at the closing ceremony on Saturday, 6 February.

  1. 01NoticeA real problem, unmet need or opportunity in Bangladesh.
  2. 02ProposeA practical, AI-driven way to solve it.
  3. 03PresentOne poster that makes the case.
