Build. Exhibit. Explain.

Participants exhibit innovative projects, prototypes and technological solutions, showing creativity, originality, practical applications and potential impact on society. Run by HACK, teams of up to three demo to judges and visitors on Friday, 5 February, from 10 am to 5 pm.
